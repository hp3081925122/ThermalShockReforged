# Thermal Shock Reforged

## English

> **Thermal Shock Reforged is an unofficial port and rewrite of [Thermal Shock](https://modrinth.com/mod/thermal-shock) by [ThermaDev](https://modrinth.com/user/ThermaDev).** All of the original world, creatures, characters, story, art, models, sounds and music are ThermaDev's work. This project is not made or endorsed by ThermaDev — please report Reforged issues here, not to the original author.

Thermal Shock Reforged is a high-version rewrite of the original Thermal Shock mod. It keeps the original's science-fiction winter dimension, strange ecosystems, exploration, structures, lore, and combat identity, while rebuilding the experience for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge with a more complete and maintainable foundation.

The current build contains 5 major interconnected story threads, 189 registered entities including creatures and combat entities, 9 key NPCs with dialogue and trades, and 4 boss-bar encounters. NPC errands, advancements, broadcasts, structure books, recordings, resource gathering, crafting, and boss fights gradually reveal the world and open the next stage of the journey.

Enter the Endless Garden (Loqendia) through a horizontal 3x3 obsidian platform and the Dimensional Keystone. Explore frozen biomes, underground facilities, abandoned settlements, gardens, sanctums, and other structures while uncovering the history of an insect civilization, the Blizzard Project, infection, lost energy technology, and the forces guiding your arrival.

### Suggested progression

1.  Sleep in the Overworld, then follow the location prompt that appears and search the indicated structure to obtain the Dimensional Keystone.
2.  Build the keystone on a flat 3x3 obsidian platform and enter the Endless Garden.
3.  Find settlements and NPCs, accept errands, collect local ores and resources, and follow advancements and broadcasts.
4.  Explore structures and story locations, read their records, learn the history of the garden, and unlock new equipment and routes.
5.  Face elite creatures and bosses, complete the major quest threads, and use the rewards to continue deeper into the dimension.

Thermal Shock Reforged is designed as a lore-heavy exploration and combat adventure: the world, encounters, structures, and story are meant to be discovered together.

## 中文

> **Thermal Shock Reforged 是 [ThermaDev](https://modrinth.com/user/ThermaDev) 所作 [Thermal Shock](https://modrinth.com/mod/thermal-shock) 的非官方移植与重写版本。** 原作的世界观、生物、角色、剧情、美术、模型、音效与音乐均为 ThermaDev 的作品。本项目并非 ThermaDev 制作或认可，Reforged 的问题请在本页反馈，不要打扰原作者。

Thermal Shock Reforged 是原版 Thermal Shock 模组的高版本重置版本。它保留原作的科幻冬季异世界、奇异生态、探索、结构、剧情和战斗特色，并针对 Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge 重新构建，让整个体验拥有更完整、更易维护的基础。

当前版本包含 5 条相互关联的主要剧情线、189 个注册实体（包括生物与战斗实体）、9 名拥有对话和交易的主要 NPC，以及 4 个带 Boss 血条的首领战。NPC 委托、进度、广播、结构内书籍、录音、资源获取、制作和首领战会逐步揭开世界真相，并引导玩家进入下一阶段。

玩家需要使用界域基石，在平放的 3×3 黑曜石平台上进入无尽花园（Loqendia）。你将在冰雪群系、地下设施、废弃聚落、花园、圣所和其他结构中探索，逐步了解虫族文明、暴雪计划、感染、失落的光能技术，以及引导你来到这里的神秘力量。

### 推荐游玩流程

1.  先在主世界睡觉，随后根据出现的地点提示，前往对应结构获取界域基石。
2.  将界域基石放在平放的 3×3 黑曜石平台上，进入无尽花园。
3.  寻找聚落和 NPC，接受委托，收集当地矿物与资源，并跟随进度和广播推进。
4.  探索结构与剧情地点，阅读记录，了解无尽花园的历史，解锁新的装备与路线。
5.  挑战精英生物和首领，完成主要剧情线，并利用奖励继续深入这个维度。

Thermal Shock Reforged 是一个重剧情的探索与战斗冒险模组；世界、遭遇、结构和故事需要玩家在探索中共同发现。
